# Witan

**Witan by [Opifex Studios](https://opifexstudios.ca)** is a free Windows manager with a private AI built in. It helps Microsoft Defender spot suspicious files, shows what's filling your drives, tames startup apps and tidies up. Ask Witan explains anything in plain words, and it runs entirely on your PC.

## Download

**[⬇ Download WitanSetup.exe](https://github.com/arkbullet2626-droid/witan-releases/releases/latest/download/WitanSetup.exe)** (always the newest version)

Older versions are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/arkbullet2626-droid/witan-releases/releases).

Witan isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your browser may also say the file isn't commonly downloaded; choose **Keep**.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- 4 GB RAM minimum; 8 GB or a graphics card recommended
- About 1 to 3 GB of free space for the AI model, downloaded once the first time Witan runs

## Privacy

No accounts, no analytics. Your files, questions and settings stay on your PC. Witan only downloads its AI model (once) and a tiny update file, and it never uploads anything. The installer adds Windows Firewall rules that keep the main app and the AI engine offline.
